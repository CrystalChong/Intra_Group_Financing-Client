Health Check Roadmap

A walkthrough of how your Intragroup Financing Health Check (RM3,888 incl. 8% SST) actually runs — what we need from you before the session, what happens during the 90 minutes, and what you'll walk away with.

Before your session

What we need from you

Please return all three items at least 48 hours before your scheduled session, so we can prepare properly.

1

Advances Movement Schedule

Your finance team populates this with opening balances, additions, settlements, and funding sources for every borrower under this lender, across YA 2023–2025.

Download the Excel template → 01A_Advances_Movement_Schedule.xlsx
2

Group Structure / Shareholding Chart

Your latest group organisational chart, so we can map the related-party relationships (subsidiaries, sister companies) and confirm the Transfer Pricing scope under s.140A. Please attach this directly to your reply email — there's no upload field for it here, since this page carries no file storage of its own.

3

Online Diagnostic Intake

Answer the essential screening questions about the lender and its borrowers, then click Print / Save PDF at the bottom of the page and attach the exported PDF to your reply email.

Open the intake questionnaire → 01_Client_Intake_Questionnaire.html
Submission deadline: reply to your onboarding email with all three items attached — the Excel schedule, the group structure chart, and your exported intake PDF — at least 48 hours before the session.
During your session

What we work through together

These are run live by your consultant using what you've submitted — nothing further is needed from you here.

4

Intra-Group Loan Decision Tree

Walks each borrower's facts through the Transfer Pricing scope, trade vs non-trade, and debt-vs-equity questions.

5

Loan vs Equity Scorecard

A detailed, criteria-by-criteria score if a borrower's debt/equity character is ambiguous.

6

Simplified Method Scorecard

Checks whether each borrower qualifies for the BNM-published FD Rate or ALR, instead of a full benchmarking study.

7

Financial Impact Study

Quantifies the illustrative deemed interest income and surcharge exposure using indicative market rates.

After your session

What you walk away with

8

Health Check Summary

A consolidated High/Medium/Low risk matrix and a clear recommendation on whether a full Impact Study and formal TP benchmarking is needed next.

Questions while compiling your figures? Just reply to your onboarding email — we're happy to help. See the overview page for links to everything.
